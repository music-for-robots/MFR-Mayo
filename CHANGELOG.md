# Changelog

## [1.0.0] - 2026-09-30

First release.

- Op-amp style fuzz (Sustain, Tone, Volume) into a cranked British amp and 4x12 cab, with an Amp + Cab bypass
- Microphonic pickup feedback loop: the whistle blooms when you stop playing (Squeal, Pitch, Distance, Drift, Bloom)
- Noise gate first in the chain (Threshold, Attack, Fade up to 5 s); silent at rest, and it sets how long the whistle rings
- INIT button, knob shortcuts (double-click/Alt-click reset, Ctrl/Shift fine drag, type-in values)
- 4x oversampled, VST3 and Standalone for Windows 64-bit
